ABOUT
CHEF JAY LeSOUL

Cooking with soul for 15+ years

Chef Jay LeSoul Catering is built on a passion for great food, real flavor, and bringing delicious meals to the community. Our mission is to create unforgettable experiences through fresh ingredients, bold flavors, and exceptional service.

James Thomas Garrett III, known as Chef Jay LeSoul, is a Cleveland-area private and celebrity chef who leads a team of 10 and created the Cooking in the Hood initiative to bring great food back to his community.

THE CHEF BEHIND THE SOUL

From the neighborhood to the plate

Chef Jay LeSoul built his name one plate at a time — private dinners, celebrity events and community cookouts across Greater Cleveland. His food is rooted in soul cooking and elevated with fresh ingredients, bold seasoning and a chef's precision.

Today he leads a team of 10 and brings the same energy to every booking, whether it's an intimate in-home dinner or a 300-guest corporate function.
