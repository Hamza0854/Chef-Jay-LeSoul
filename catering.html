How We Serve

Four ways to work with Chef Jay

Whether it's a table for two or two hundred, every engagement starts with a conversation about your event, your guests, and the flavors you want on the plate.

Signature Dinner

A soulful, seasonal tasting menu built around premium proteins and artisanal sides — every plate finished with our signature Black Crack Seasoning for a bold, warm flavor profile.

Private In-Home

Bring the kitchen to your doorstep. We offer polished, intimate service for private dinners and small gatherings, handling setup through cleanup so you can stay with your guests.

Event & Wedding Catering

Full-service catering for corporate events, weddings, and community celebrations. We manage logistics, staffing, and service for a polished, professional experience at any scale.

Custom Menu Options

Every event is unique. We work with you to build a custom menu — dietary requirements, regional flavor profiles, or a signature dish you've always wanted served — brought to life.
